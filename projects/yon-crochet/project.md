Yon Crochet started with a simple idea: **anyone can learn to crochet if the first steps feel fun.** I co-founded the business and lead everything people see — the brand, the product experience and the marketing.

I produced 1,000+ multimedia assets — packaging, tutorials, blogs and social visuals — using Adobe Creative Suite, Figma, Canva and CapCut, keeping one consistent identity across every platform. I also revitalised the Shopify storefront and pitched the business to two funding panels, winning both.
